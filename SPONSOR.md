# Sponsor Awesome Codex CLI

One labeled sponsor slot at the top of the [README](README.md), seen by developers looking for Codex CLI tools.

- **Price:** $29 / month, paid upfront
- **Includes:** one text line — product name, link, and a one-line description (max ~150 characters)
- **Slots:** 1 at a time, first come first served

## How to book

1. Email **sponsor@milisp.dev** (or DM [@lisp_mi](https://x.com/lisp_mi)) with your product link and the line you want shown.
2. Once approved, you'll receive a private payment link.
3. Your line goes live within 48 hours of payment.
4. Payments made without prior approval will be refunded.

## Rules

- Sponsorship is labeled "Sponsor" and does not affect inclusion in the curated list.
- The maintainer may decline any sponsor. Not accepted: token/API resellers, relay or proxy APIs, account sales.
- The description must be factual; no marketing superlatives.
